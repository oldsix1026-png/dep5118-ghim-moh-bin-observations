# ITA3 project plan

## Scope

Ghim Moh Gardens public spaces and the outdoor walking route toward Blk 20 Ghim Moh Road Market and Food Centre. Exclude bins inside the food centre, private premises, and residential doorways. The study concerns public-bin overflow observations, not formal service complaints.

## Participants and data users

- Contributors: residents and people using the public walking routes.
- Project manager/moderator: Zhou Jiaxuan for the class prototype.
- Potential outreach partner: Ghim Moh Gardens Residents' Committee. Not a confirmed partner.
- Potential data user: Holland-Bukit Panjang Town Council. Not a confirmed partner.
- Urgent clean-up channel: OneService, outside this prototype.

## One-minute form

Required: public location marker and condition (`Nearly full`, `Full`, `Litter outside`). Ushahidi also requires a title and description; these have neutral prefilled values that contributors can edit. The platform records submission time automatically; the form does not ask for a separate observation time. Optional: photograph. No name, phone number, email, or residential unit number requested.

The form map currently opens at the platform's default location because saving a deployment-wide map centre requires a public contact email. Search `20 Ghim Moh Road Singapore` to reach the study area, then move the marker to the actual public bin. Do not submit the market address as a bin location without checking it.

## Management

Use a four-week trial as a proposal, not a claim of actual fieldwork. A moderator checks that each submission is in scope, removes accidental duplicates of the same observation, and screens optional photographs for faces, number plates, and home entrances before publication. Independent observations of the same bin can remain, with their different times recorded. Public points may be rounded if the platform can do so without obscuring a public-bin location. Publish only moderated reports. Separate the 25 synthetic teaching records at 10 illustrative sites from any live submissions and label both datasets prominently.

## Story for the map

The question is not how many bins exist. It is whether reports recur at the same public points and at similar or different times of day. The map aggregates 25 synthetic observations at 10 illustrative sites; it demonstrates repeated-report visualization and filtering, not a real pattern. Any real inference requires actual observations over the trial period.

## Submission checklist

- One PDF with objective (max 150 words), community partners (max 100 words), and data gathering/management plan (max 150 words).
- Working public website URL (not just the repository URL).
- A form that an unauthenticated visitor can actually submit.
- Participant manual with screenshots.
- Visualization with at least five submissions; synthetic records clearly marked.
- Incognito submission tested; screenshot of the confirmation state.
