Participant guide

What counts as a report?

Report a public bin in Ghim Moh Gardens or along the walking route to the Blk 20 market and food centre. The report should describe what you can see now, not a guess about how often it happens.

If possible, place the marker at the bin rather than at your home. You do not need to give your name or contact details. The project moderator checks locations, removes accidental repeat submissions of the same observation, and screens optional photos before publishing. Separate observations of the same bin can remain as separate reports.

Need a clean-up rather than a map record? Report the issue to OneService.

Purpose and limits

A small-area picture, not a cleanliness score

A single full bin may be cleared quickly. Reports across different days and times could reveal recurring pressure along a route, giving residents and potential local partners a clearer starting point for discussion.

This is an independent student prototype. Ghim Moh Gardens Residents' Committee and Holland-Bukit Panjang Town Council are potential outreach or data users, not confirmed project partners. Public reports are observations, not proof of a management failure.
