Why repeated reports matter

A full bin can make a shared space harder to use. When neighbours note where and when it fills up, repeated reports can show which places need a closer look. After checking them on site, cleaning teams could adjust collection times or consider moving or adding bins. The goal is a cleaner neighbourhood for everyone.
