Why repeated reports matter

One full bin may be cleared quickly. Reports from the same place on different days can show whether the problem keeps returning or happens at particular times. That can help residents explain the issue and give service teams a place and time to check.

Reports are observations, not proof that a bin has been missed. They need checking on site and do not guarantee a clean-up. For a problem needing attention now, please send an official request through OneService.
